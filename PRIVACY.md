# Tracevelle privacy

Draft for release review · 2026-09-27

Tracevelle processes selected patterns on your device. It has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, cloud service or external image-processing API. Imported originals and project settings are stored in the app's local storage and may be included in your device's system backup. Photos access uses Apple's selected-item picker; full photo-library access is not requested.

Apple processes purchases. Tracevelle uses verified StoreKit transactions to determine Lifetime Pro access. The developer does not receive payment-card details through the app.

Email support is optional. If you email us, your email and anything you choose to attach are used to respond. Avoid sending sensitive patterns unnecessarily.

Deleting the app removes its local app data; system backups are managed through your device settings. Named patterns can be removed from the library. Privacy/support contact: Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir, hello@arvinify.com, https://www.arvinify.com.

App Store privacy responses and a hosted privacy URL remain to be configured and verified.
